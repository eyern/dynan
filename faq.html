What is the typical timeline for a residential construction project?

A custom home typically takes 6 to 12 months, depending on complexity, site conditions, and permitting.

Do you handle the permitting and approval process?

Yes, our in-house team handles all building permits, zoning approvals, and local authority inspections to ensure compliance.t

What costs are involved in buying a new home beyond the purchase price?

Buyers should budget for down payments, closing costs, legal fees, and potential property tax adjustments.

Can I customize my home during the construction phase?

Yes, we offer customization options for finishes, paint, and layout tweaks, provided they are requested before the installation phase, usually allowing for a "pre-construction" customization period.

How do you estimate profitability for a new project?

We conduct a thorough analysis of acquisition costs, construction costs, financing, market trends, and projected revenue to determine ROI

What steps are taken to ensure the quality of construction?

We employ strict quality control measures, including professional site supervisors, site inspections, and using only approved materials.

How long has your company been in operation?

We have been developing projects in the Nairobi & Coastal Region area for over 10 years, focusing on delivering high-quality residential and commercial spaces.

How do you verify the market demand for a new project?

We use data-driven market research, including local demographic trends, competitive analysis, and economic drivers, to ensure the project meets market demand.
